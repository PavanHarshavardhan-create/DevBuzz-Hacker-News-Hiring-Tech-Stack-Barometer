How the barometer works

Simple counts. Clear signals.

DevBuzz scans the public Hacker News hiring threads and counts which technology names appear in each job post.

01

Collect

Find monthly “Who is Hiring?” threads and read their direct replies, which are the job postings.

02

Detect

Match technology names and aliases from a dictionary. Repeated mentions count once per posting.

03

Compare

Calculate the share of valid postings mentioning each technology, then compare it with earlier months.

The main percentage

Post share = job posts that mention a technology ÷ all valid job posts in that month.

One post can mention several technologies, so percentages across technologies can add up to more than 100%.
What data can I import?

Use the backend’s monthly_share.json or monthly_share.csv for charts and rankings. You can also select several pipeline CSVs together: postings.csv, posting_tech.csv, and tech_dictionary.csv. The page will use posting text for the feed and can calculate monthly shares from the posting/technology pairs.

The backend does not currently extract company, salary, or structured work-mode labels. When imported post text is available, the dashboard estimates work mode from phrases; treat those counts as a heuristic. The sample report labels itself clearly, and imported posts show only fields present in the data.
